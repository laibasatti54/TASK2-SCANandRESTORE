Terms of Service

Last updated September 29, 2026

Using the service

Halide Lab scans, restores and stores film images that you send us or upload. You confirm that you own the images or have permission to use them, and that they do not break the law.

Plans and billing

Plans renew each month or each year, depending on the billing period you choose. You can cancel at any time from your account. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the end of the paid period.

Your files

You keep all rights to your images. We receive a limited license to process and store them only to provide the service you ordered. Physical film is returned within 30 days of scanning unless you ask us to recycle it.

Refunds

If a scan does not meet the quality described on your plan, contact us within 14 days and we will redo it or refund that order.

Changes

We may update these terms. We will post the new version here and email active customers before material changes take effect.

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

What we collect

Your name, email, shipping address and payment details, plus the images you send or upload. Payment card numbers are handled by our payment processor and never stored on our servers.

How we use it

To scan, restore, store and return your work, to process payments, and to contact you about your orders. We do not sell your images or personal data, and we do not use your images to train models.

Sharing

We share data only with providers that help run the service, such as payment, shipping and storage companies, under agreements that limit their use of it.

Retention and deletion

We keep your images while your plan is active and for 60 days after it ends. You can export or delete your images and account at any time from settings, or by writing to privacy@halidelab.example.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.
